## Blocking issues (deduplicated across the five)

**B1 (5/5) - `morph` does not read `pager.backMorph` on a tap; deleting the scrub makes the design inert.** `Header.svelte:146-185`. The only read of `pager.backMorph` inside `morph` is in the `dragging` arm (`:150`). On a tap `pager.dragging === false`; the settle arm (`:167-176`) needs `settling` or a release frame and reads `settleProgress`/`lastGestureMorph` anyway; the rest arm (`:181`) returns `currentHasTabs ? 1 : 0` and does not consult `backMorph`. So with the scrub branch (`:156-159`) deleted and no replacement, `morph` jumps at the route-land flush; `searchProgress` and `tabProgress` jump in lockstep; the ENTER and MIRROR tests regress to the pre-DV08 parallel-slide shape. §4.4 item 3 ("morph flows through the existing arms reading `backMorph`") is contradicted by §9 item 6 itself.

**B2 (4/5) - the GPL pager `$effect` clobbers the tap rAF's `backMorph`.** `GesturePageLayout.svelte:344-443`, `mobile-pager.svelte.ts:58-65`. The deep-branch `$effect` publishes `pager.set` on every tracked-dep change: the rest branch writes `backMorph: 0` (`:435-442`), the committed branch writes `backMorph: 1` (`:426-433`). `set` writes all fields atomically; there is no field-level setter. On a `/` → `/search` tap, navigation land flips `navInFlight`/`pendingNav`/`isTransitioningOut`, re-running the `$effect` and overwriting whatever the tap rAF just wrote. The rAF and the `$effect` fight on every flush.

**B3 (4/5) - the Header consumer CSS-transition gates lose their suppression without `searchScrubbing`.** `Header.svelte:205-207, 638, 650, 657`. Today `trackStyle`/`searchButtonStyle`/`tabBarStyle` gate on `dragging || searchScrubbing || navStore.navInFlight`, and `slideT` on `dragging || searchScrubbing`. With `searchScrubbing` deleted, on a tap (`dragging=false`, `navInFlight` only briefly true) the CSS `transform/left/max-height 200ms ease-out` runs while the rAF also publishes continuous values; the two fight, reopening the desync inside the Header itself. The plan names no replacement gate.

**B4 (3/5) - `iconProgress` (`Header.svelte:194`) references `searchScrubbing` and is missed by §5.** `const iconProgress = $derived(isSearch || (searchScrubbing && currentHasTabs) ? 0 : 1 - morph)`. Deleting `searchScrubbing` is a compile error here, and without an equivalent freeze the icon visibly morphs hamburger ↔ back-arrow during the tap (pre-land `isSearch=false`, `morph` interpolates, so `iconProgress = 1 - morph` sweeps). §5 lists `slideT`/`trackStyle`/`tabBarStyle`/`searchButtonStyle` but omits `iconProgress` and mis-cites `searchButtonStyle` (`:650`, not `:638`/`:657`).

**B5 (3/5) - the tap rAF arming points are under-specified, and the exit direction has no viable publisher.** `GesturePageLayout.svelte:237-244` (`shouldAnimateEnter`, forward-only), `:770-795` (cross-tab chip-exit), `:797-822` (same-panel exit), `:848-871` (onDestroy), `MobileTabPager.svelte:91,107,140-141` (always `backMorph: null`). §4.4 item 1 names only the forward enter and the cross-tab path. The `/search → /` back-tap actually routes through the same-panel exit branch (`:797-822`), which is a third arming point the plan never names. Worse, on that direction the `/search` GPL unmounts at route land and `/` mounts `MobileTabPager` (forcing `backMorph: null`), so the only window for a `/search`-GPL rAF is the ~200ms same-panel slide before `onTrackTransitionEnd` dispatches. The plan does not compose the rAF with `pendingNav`/`onTrackTransitionEnd`/`startPendingNavPoll`.

**B6 (3/5) - GPL cannot cleanly identify `/search`; the organic-clean claim is unverifiable.** `route-config.ts:81-83` (`/search` has no `getParent`), `header-mode.ts:20-24` (`resolveHeaderMode`). GPL today has zero `isSearch`/`resolveHeaderMode`/`/search` references. `currentHasTabs` alone cannot discriminate root↔search from root↔deep (22+ deep routes also flip it), so arming on it alone regresses those routes. The only discriminator is `isSearch`, which requires `resolveHeaderMode` (a `/search` literal behind a helper) or adding `getParent` to `/search` (blast radius across `DEEP_ROUTES` consumers). §4.6 asserts "no `/search` token" without naming the detector.

**B7 (1/5, promoted from non-blocking) - the Page slide actually becomes ~83ms.** Because the headroom term makes the Page slide occupy `morph [0.2, 1]`, and the scrub eases `morph = (1-t)^3`, the Page slide runs only the first 83ms of the rAF then idles 117ms while the Tab expands. §4.5 explicitly rejects "speed the Page slide to match the 83ms track" as regressing enter feel, yet the Round-0 design produces exactly that 83ms Page slide. The plan cannot both own this design and keep §4.5's rejection.

## Notable concerns (non-blocking)

- **Effect E's full state.** §5 says "five state fields" but Effect E also owns `prevHadTabs`/`prevSearchTitle`/`prevIsSearch` (`Header.svelte:125-127`); `prevPath`/`lastPath`/`prevHasTabs` (`:128-135`) STAY (Effect C at `:348`). The deletion list must separate these.
- **§4.4 item 4 vs §5 contradiction.** "SearchTabBar / search button / trackStyle: unchanged" (§4.4) versus "adjust the gates that reference `searchScrubbing`" (§5). Both cannot hold.
- **SSR / enter-mount flash.** On `/` → `/search` the `/search` GPL mounts fresh; the proposed GPL rAF can fire only after `onMount`, at least one render later than Effect E (which runs in `$effect.pre` same-flush). In the gap `morph` evaluates to rest and may flash `1 → 0 → 1 → 0`. §6 case 5 covers only deep-link.
- **`coverProgress` / FAB.** `/search` is overlay family (`route-config.ts:81-83`); `FloatingActionButtonLayer.svelte:399` reads `pager.coverProgress`. A tap rAF calling `pager.set` without `coverProgress` nulls it every frame (set defaults omitted fields to null). §5 omits `FloatingActionButtonLayer` and §9 never mentions `coverProgress`.
- **`MobileTabPager` rest value.** `MobileTabPager` always publishes `backMorph: null`, so §3.8's "0 at client rest" holds only on deep routes, not on `/`. The source-side value a `/` → `/search` rAF interpolates from is `null`.
- **`isMobile` gating of the rAF.** The rAF arming site is not conditionally gated; on desktop a tap rAF would publish the tap signal for the first time from a non-mobile path.
- **e2e sync assertion may pass spuriously.** Under the broken design both the Page slide (CSS `duration-200` on `snapIndex`) and the Header track (CSS `transform 200ms` on nav land) run independent CSS of equal nominal duration; `|trackNorm − pageNorm| < 0.2` could pass by coincidence. The assertion must verify rAF-driven 1:1 behavior (tighter tolerance, single-frame), not just co-finishing.
- **`visualDragOffset` clamp.** §3.1 drops `Math.max(0, ...)` (`GesturePageLayout.svelte:452`); the clamp is structural (it creates the headroom segment). A `tapVisualOffset` must mirror it.
- **§3.4** says "three sources"; the code has four branches (dragging, searchScrubbing, settle, rest). Cosmetic.

## Organic-clean

4/5 "not clean", 1/5 "borderline". The Header-side deletion (Effect E + `startSearchScrub` + five state fields + the `searchScrubbing` gates) is a genuine net reduction in `/search` coupling inside a shared primitive. The GPL-side addition cannot be evaluated because the detector is unspecified (B6); the most direct implementation (a `/search` literal) violates §4.6, and the cleanest (`resolveHeaderMode` import) is a semantic coupling relocated from Header to GPL - defensible only if documented as a GPL → `header-mode` dependency, not as "GPL untouched by search-awareness".

## Revision decisions (applied to `docs/DV17-Plan.md` as Round 1)

1. **Independent `tapMorph` field, not `backMorph` reuse (resolves B1, B2, the FAB `coverProgress` concern).** `mobile-pager.svelte.ts` gains a `tapMorph: number | null` field (`null` = no tap scrub in flight). GPL's tap rAF is its sole writer; the existing drag `$effect` never writes it. `morph` gains a new arm `if (pager.tapMorph !== null) return pager.tapMorph` placed between the drag arm and the settle arm. This reads the tap signal without setting `dragging: true` (no MobileTabBar / titleView / FAB / slideT side effects) and without the scrub branch. `set` preserves `tapMorph` when the field is omitted by the drag `$effect` (so drag publishes do not clobber an in-flight tap scrub; the tap rAF cancels before any drag arms anyway).
2. **Linear easing on the tap path (resolves B7).** The tap rAF interpolates `tapMorph` linearly between the two rest values (matching the drag, where `morph = backMorph` is linear in the finger). `searchProgress` then occupies `morph [0.2, 1]` = the linear-time first 80% ≈ 160ms, not 83ms. The Page slide and the Header track both run ~160ms and are synchronized; the residual gap to 200ms is the cost of keeping `slide-then-expand` segmentation, acknowledged in §4.5.
3. **Replace the `searchScrubbing` gates with `tapMorph !== null` (resolves B3, B4).** `slideT`, `trackStyle`, `searchButtonStyle`, `tabBarStyle` gate on `dragging || tapMorph !== null || navStore.navInFlight`. `iconProgress` freezes on `tapMorph !== null` (the icon stays a hamburger across the root↔search tap, as today). §5 adds `iconProgress` and corrects the `searchButtonStyle` line.
4. **Name all three arming points; the exit publishes before unmount (resolves B5).** Enter: the `/search` GPL `onMount`, gated on forward nav + an `isSearch` flip. Exit: the `/search` GPL same-panel `beforeNavigate` branch (`:797-822`), which already owns the ~200ms slide window before `onTrackTransitionEnd` dispatches; the rAF runs there and publishes `tapMorph 0 → 1` over that window. Cross-tab chip-exit does not arm (target ≠ root). `MobileTabPager` forcing `backMorph: null` on `/` mount does not affect `tapMorph`.
5. **GPL detects `/search` via `resolveHeaderMode` (resolves B6).** GPL imports `resolveHeaderMode` and evaluates it for the current path and the previous path (`navStore.activeStack[len-2].pathname`); a root↔search tap is the `isSearch` flip. No literal `/search` token enters GPL; §4.6 is rewritten to document this as a GPL → `header-mode` semantic dependency (a coupling relocated from Header's deleted Effect E, net-neutral to cleaner).
6. **Page slide headroom via `tapVisualOffset` mirroring `visualDragOffset` (with the `Math.max(0,…)` clamp).** `trackTranslateX` gains a `tapMorph !== null` branch ahead of the `snapIndex` fallback; the offset uses the same `HEADER_MORPH_THRESHOLD` headroom as `visualDragOffset`. CSS `duration-200` is suppressed while `tapMorph !== null` (parallel gate to `:475`) and re-enabled when it clears; the flag is cleared in the same cleanup that arms a drag (no strand).
7. **SSR / flash / isMobile.** The `tapMorph` arm falls through to rest when `tapMorph === null`, so SSR and deep-link render at rest. The rAF arms only on an actual navigation (forward enter with a stack, or same-panel back), never on a deep-link reload. The arming site is `isMobile`-gated; desktop never publishes `tapMorph`.
8. **Full Effect E deletion list.** Delete `searchScrubbing`, `searchScrubProgress`, `searchScrubFrom`, `searchScrubTo`, `searchScrubRafId`, `prevHadTabs`, `prevSearchTitle`, `prevIsSearch`, Effect E (`:402-432`), and `startSearchScrub` (`:434-457`). Keep `prevPath`/`lastPath`/`prevHasTabs` (Effect C consumers).
9. **Strengthen the e2e sync assertion** to verify rAF-driven 1:1 behavior (tighter tolerance, single-frame delta), with a CALIBRATION that fails on master.

Open for Round 2 (the §9 UNVERIFIED list, narrowed): the `tapMorph` arm's interaction with Effect A's `lastGestureMorph` latch and Effect C's settle (a tap must not arm a phantom settle); the exit-direction rAF timing inside the `beforeNavigate` same-panel window vs `onTrackTransitionEnd` dispatch; whether 160ms (linear) is acceptable as the synchronized Page/track duration or whether the segmentation threshold itself must be revisited; and the `resolveHeaderMode` import's organic-clean standing.
